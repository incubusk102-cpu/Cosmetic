## Pre-flight findings

- **Font stack:** Inter (body) + IBM Plex Serif (display), via `next/font` variables `--font-inter` and `--font-plex-serif`. `tailwind.config.ts:37-40`. **2+1 rule: respected** (no third family on the page).
- **Palette:** custom CSS properties mirrored from Tailwind, anchored on muted sage `#5C7A6A`. `landing.css:14-22`. Locked to named tokens for everything except a couple of inline-style escapes (see Major #4 below).
- **Motion:** **motion-cut project.** No `framer-motion`, `gsap`, `motion`, `lenis`, `@react-spring`, or `lottie-react` in `package.json`. CSS-only transitions, with a `prefers-reduced-motion` shutoff (`landing.css:511-516`). Good baseline.
- **Spacing:** ad-hoc rem values — no `--space-*` scale, no Tailwind `extend.spacing`. Most values land on a 4 pt grid but several drift (`0.55rem` / `0.6rem` / `0.85rem` / `1.075rem` / `1.4rem`).
- **Framework:** Next.js 14 App Router. Component is server-rendered, no client JS on the marketing route — strong LCP posture.
- **Genre (inferred from PLAN.md §8 mood and the executed palette):** **modern-minimal with an editorial inflection.** "Closer to Stripe Atlas or Linear than to Sephora" per the founder. Audit grades against modern-minimal allowances (e.g., `#fff` paper-raised is OK; gradient text is NOT).

Hallmark will preserve: font stack, palette tokens, motion-cut posture, prefers-reduced-motion fallback.

## Findings

### Critical (ships as slop)

**[critical] AI structural template — fingerprint of the LLM landing page**
`Landing.tsx:131-422` · gate **9** (anti-patterns § *Default-attractor sameness*)
The macrostructure is *Hero (left text + right mock) → 3 equal feature cards → 3 equal numbered steps → 2-tier pricing → 3 equal trust columns → centred final CTA → footer*. That's the default LLM rhythm — every section is a triplet, every triplet is the same column-count, every section heading sits in the same position. The page reads as a template even though each individual section is well-built.
→ **Fix.** Replace one of the triplets with a different shape. Three options, pick one: (a) drop "How it works" entirely — the steps duplicate the feature cards' content; (b) convert "Trust" into a long-document letter-close (`Ft6` archetype) instead of a 3-up; (c) convert "Features" into an asymmetric **Bento Grid** (one big tile + two small ones) so the rhythm breaks. Even one of these would defeat the fingerprint.

**[critical] The 3-column feature grid (icon-tile feature card)**
`Landing.tsx:207-240`, `landing.css:305-342`, `landing.css:494-499` · anti-patterns § *The 3-column feature grid* + § *Icon-tile feature card*
Three equal columns, each with an `accent-tint` square holding a Lucide stroke icon above a serif heading above 3 lines of body. This is the most-recognised AI feature pattern.
→ **Fix.** Break the grid. Either mix column widths (`1.6fr 1fr 1fr`), drop the icons inline with the heading instead of stacking, vary card heights, or collapse to typographic rhythm (no cards, just hanging headings with a short body).

**[critical] Repeated structural fingerprint — 3-step flow mirrors 3-feature grid**
`Landing.tsx:257-291`, `landing.css:345-383, 496` · gate **9**
The "How it works" section is the same 3-column-at-≥720px grid as Features, just with numbers instead of icons. Two consecutive sections sharing the same fingerprint reads as templating.
→ **Fix.** Restructure as a numbered **Long Document** with hanging numerals in a left margin, body running full-width to the right — or cut the section, since the three features already imply the loop.

### Major (looks AI-generated)

**[major] Eyebrow on every section**
`Landing.tsx:136, 163, 176, 198, 248, 299, 390`; `landing.css:39-45` · anti-patterns § *Eyebrow on every section*
Seven eyebrows on one page (`Cosmetic Allergy Tracker`, `Scan result`, `What it does`, `Ingredients (first 6)`, `How it works`, `Plans`, `Ready`). The rule is "default OFF" and "cap at 1–2 per page" even when justified. None of these sections are ordinal or chaptered.
→ **Fix.** Keep at most one — probably the hero's `Cosmetic Allergy Tracker` since it doubles as a wordmark hint. Delete the rest; let the H2s stand on their own.

**[major] Hand-drawn UI mock anchoring the hero**
`Landing.tsx:159-190`, `landing.css:191-276` · anti-patterns § *Re-drawn UI chrome*
The "Calm Day Cream / Caution / Lift 1.6×" card is a hand-built mock of the actual verdict screen. The real screen exists in the product (`app/(app)/scan/result/[id]/page.tsx`) and would be more truthful than a drawn approximation. The mock also carries an invented metric ("Lift in your history · 1.6×") and an invented product name ("Calm Day Cream / La Brève Cosmetics").
→ **Fix.** Replace with a real screenshot of the verdict screen, wrapped in `<figure>` with a hairline border. If that's not available, drop the mock entirely and let the H1 carry the hero (E1 typographic anchor from `hero-enrichment.md`).

**[major] Uniform hover translate + shadow lift on every feature card**
`landing.css:316-321` · gate **14** (anti-patterns § *Universal hover:scale-105*)
`.feature-card:hover` simultaneously runs `transform: translateY(-2px)` + `box-shadow: var(--shadow-lift)` + a `transition: transform 200ms, box-shadow 200ms`. Two effects at once, applied uniformly across three sibling cards.
→ **Fix.** Pick one signal. Either drop the translate and keep the shadow shift, or drop the shadow shift and keep a 1 px border-colour deepen. Never both.

**[major] Centred-everything final CTA**
`Landing.tsx:388-421` · anti-patterns § *Centred everything*
`text-align: center`, eyebrow centred, headline centred, both buttons centred, inline style chain. After 800 px of left-biased layout above, the symmetry of the final CTA breaks the page's rhythm to match the AI template.
→ **Fix.** Move the final CTA into a **Ft6 letter-close** footer archetype — left-aligned, signed off, single inline link. Or anchor it to the left with the same hanging-indent rhythm as the hero.

**[major] Missing Hallmark macrostructure stamp**
`landing.css:1-9` · gate **21**
The CSS opens with a project-local descriptive comment, not the `/* Hallmark · macrostructure: <name> · genre: <name> · theme: <name> */` stamp Hallmark requires. The skill can't tell what was picked, can't enforce diversification, can't audit drift.
→ **Fix.** Once a Hallmark redesign runs, stamp the chosen macrostructure + genre + theme + pre-emit critique scores at the top of `landing.css`.

### Minor (small taste issues)

**[minor] Inline-style colour and font escapes**
`Landing.tsx:332-335, 388, 392-397, 401, 406-412, 432, 434` · gate **58** (anti-patterns § *Mid-render token improvisation*)
Final CTA section uses inline `style={{}}` props with `fontSize: "clamp(...)"`, `color: "#374E42"` (hex, not `var(--accent-ink)`), `fontStyle: "italic"`, `display: "inline-flex"`. The pricing pill has inline `marginLeft / verticalAlign`.
→ **Fix.** Lift each inline value into a class in `landing.css`. The one hex (`#374E42`) is already `var(--accent-ink)` — just reference it.

**[minor] Straight apostrophes via `&apos;`**
`Landing.tsx:145, 154, 201-204, 215, 225, 227, 245, 251, 264, 271, 275, 283, 287, 301, 307, 317, 334, 370, 378, 382, 399, 432` · anti-patterns § *Straight quotes*
~22 instances of `&apos;` rendering as a straight `'`. Hallmark wants `'` (U+2019).
→ **Fix.** Replace `&apos;` with the typographic apostrophe `'` in JSX. The mirror at `landing/index.html` likely needs the same.

**[minor] Radial-bloom decoration behind the mock card**
`landing.css:196-205` · gate **31** (anti-patterns § *Aurora-blob background*)
A 60 % radial gradient in `rgba(92,122,106,0.16)` blooms behind the mock card. Modern-minimal does not allow radial blooms by default (only atmospheric does). The bloom is small and tasteful, but it's the lightest version of an anti-pattern.
→ **Fix.** Either drop the gradient and let the mock's shadow carry depth on its own, OR move the genre to *atmospheric* (which carries other obligations — see `references/genres/atmospheric.md`).

**[minor] AA contrast — primary button copy on accent**
`landing.css:65` · gate **46-50**
White text (`#FFFFFF`) on accent (`#5C7A6A`) computes ≈ 4.0:1 — passes WCAG 2.1 for **large** text (≥18 px) and UI components (3:1), fails for body (4.5:1). Primary buttons render at 15-16 px (`btn` 0.95 rem, `btn-lg` 1 rem).
→ **Fix.** Either deepen the accent toward `--accent-ink #374E42` for primary backgrounds (ratio ≈ 8:1, comfortable AAA), or use `--accent` only at icon-tint scales (which is what `--accent-tint` is already for) and route the primary CTA through a darker brand colour.

**[minor] Pill caution copy contrast**
`landing.css:86-89` · gate **46-50**
`color: #B8861B` on a `rgba(184,134,27,0.12)` tint over warm cream computes ≈ 3.7:1 at the 0.72 rem (~11.5 px) pill text size. Pills are decorative-ish, but a "Caution" pill that's hard to read is a credibility leak on a *medical-adjacent* product.
→ **Fix.** Darken the pill text to a deeper amber (`#7C5A12` or similar) — still verdict-amber in palette but enough contrast for small-text AA.

**[minor] Ad-hoc spacing values**
`landing.css` (throughout) · gate **26**
Several rem values fall off a 4 pt grid: `0.55rem` (8.8 px), `0.6rem` (9.6 px), `0.85rem` (13.6 px), `1.075rem` (17.2 px), `1.4rem` (22.4 px). Hallmark wants a named `--space-*` scale.
→ **Fix.** Add a 6-step scale (`--space-3xs` 4 px / `--space-2xs` 8 px / `--space-xs` 12 px / `--space-s` 16 px / `--space-m` 24 px / `--space-l` 32 px / `--space-xl` 48 px / `--space-2xl` 64 px / `--space-3xl` 96 px) to `landing.css:11-30` and migrate the loose rem values. Same scale should flow into `tailwind.config.ts theme.extend.spacing` so the in-app screens align.

## Summary

**3 critical · 5 major · 5 minor**

**Verdict — reads as AI-generated.** The execution is clean (motion-cut, locked palette, real fonts, real disclaimer, no purple-gradient hero) but the **structural fingerprint** is the canonical LLM landing-page shape: hero → 3 features → 3 steps → 2-tier pricing → 3 trust columns → CTA → footer. Fix the three Critical findings (especially #1 — break the triplet rhythm in at least one section) and the page jumps to "close, fix the minors." Until the rhythm changes, the page reads as templated even though each section is well-crafted in isolation.

The Major findings are mostly mechanical (`&apos;` → `'`, drop the eyebrow tic, prefer real screenshot over hand-drawn mock) and don't require a redesign. The Critical findings do.

## Recommended next moves (in order)

4. **Add `--space-*` token scale to `landing.css` and mirror to `tailwind.config.ts`** — sets up future Hallmark runs to inherit the same spacing language across the app.
